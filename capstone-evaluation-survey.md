## INTERPRETATION OF RESULTS

The evaluation results show a **Grand Mean of 4.46**, interpreted as **Agree**, indicating that the system meets the quality standards expected of a functional and deployable barangay information management system. The highest-rated characteristic was **Reliability (4.60 — Strongly Agree)**, reflecting the system's consistent and accurate handling of data, access control, and workflow enforcement. **Portability (4.53 — Strongly Agree)** also received high marks, confirming that the system can operate across different browsers and hosting environments.

**Efficiency (4.29 — Agree)** received the lowest mean among all characteristics, consistent with evaluator feedback noting that the GIS map rendering and PDF generation times, while acceptable, have room for optimization. **Functionality (4.42)**, **Usability (4.48)**, and **Maintainability (4.42)** all fall within the Agree range, affirming that the system is feature-complete, user-friendly, and well-structured for future enhancements.

Overall, the evaluation results confirm that the **Digital Residents for Pioneering and Information Management System for Barangay Taruc** successfully meets its development objectives and is suitable for barangay operational use.
