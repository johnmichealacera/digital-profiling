# ABSTRACT

**Title:** Digital Residents for Pioneering and Information Management System for Barangay Taruc

**Researchers:** Raizy-Maricole C. Joaquino, Rizalyn D. Consigna, Kisha Mae D. Cubillanes

**Degree:** Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

**Institution:** Bucas Grande Foundation College, College of Information Technology
Barangay Taruc, Socorro, Surigao del Norte

**Adviser:** Elealeh Jay Consigna

**Date:** December 2025

---

Local government units in the Philippines, particularly at the barangay level, play a critical role in public service delivery, yet many continue to depend on manual and paper-based systems that result in inefficiencies, data inconsistency, and slow document processing. Barangay Albino Taruc, Socorro, Surigao del Norte is among these communities where the absence of a centralized digital platform limits the effectiveness of administrative operations across resident profiling, document issuance, blotter recording, and disaster response. This study aimed to design, develop, and evaluate a comprehensive web-based information management system for Barangay Taruc covering fourteen (14) administrative modules with role-based access control for six (6) user roles. The Rapid Application Development (RAD) methodology was employed through iterative development cycles, and the system was built using Next.js 16, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, and NextAuth.js, then evaluated against the ISO 9126-1 software quality model using a five-point Likert scale administered to barangay officials, staff, and IT experts. The resulting system successfully automated the generation of twelve (12) official barangay document types in PDF format, integrated an interactive GIS household map, and enforced structured workflows for document processing, blotter case management, and disaster preparedness. Evaluation results indicate that the system meets acceptable standards across all six quality characteristics — Functionality, Efficiency, Usability, Reliability, Maintainability, and Portability — demonstrating its potential to modernize barangay governance and improve public service delivery in Barangay Albino Taruc.
